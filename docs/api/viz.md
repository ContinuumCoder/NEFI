## `nefi.viz.isosurface`

Shaded isosurfaces, one per ``projection="3d"`` axes, with one camera and one box.

Multi-level contours of GT (solid) and reconstruction (dashed) on the central slices.

### `def has_marching_cubes() -> bool`
